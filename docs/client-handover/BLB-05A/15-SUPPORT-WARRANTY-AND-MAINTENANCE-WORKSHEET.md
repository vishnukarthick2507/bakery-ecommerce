## 5. Severity classification proposal (for discussion)

| Severity | Example | Proposed handling — not an agreed SLA |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Site unavailable, suspected security incident, widespread order/payment impact | Immediate escalation through the agreed emergency channel |
| High | Checkout or admin workflow materially impaired for multiple users | Prioritized triage and frequent updates |
| Medium | A key feature degraded with a workaround | Scheduled investigation and progress updates |
| Low | Cosmetic issue, content correction or general question | Normal support queue |

The parties must approve or replace these definitions and specify actual response commitments.

## 6. Warranty and defect policy — decision required

The parties must define:

- Warranty start date and duration, if any.
- What counts as a defect against the agreed scope.
- Exclusions (for example, third-party provider outages, unauthorized modifications, unsupported environments or client-supplied content), if agreed.
- How defects are reported and what evidence is required.
- Whether fixes, workarounds and third-party escalations are included.
- Whether new features, design changes, data correction and content entry are chargeable changes.
- Warranty contact and escalation route.

**Current status:** no warranty duration, exclusions, fee, guarantee or contractual promise is supplied by this worksheet.

## 7. Maintenance and change control

Agree who is responsible for:

- Dependency/security updates and supported runtime versions.
- Hosting, domain, TLS certificates and DNS renewals.
- Database monitoring, backup policy, restore testing and access reviews.
- Provider account billing, credentials, quotas and webhook configuration.
- Incident response, log retention and security notifications.
- Browser/device compatibility review.
- Content/catalogue updates and seasonal changes.
- Change requests, estimates, approvals and release windows.
- End-of-life/decommissioning and data export/deletion.

Any production access or change must use a named account, least privilege, an approved change record and a documented rollback plan.

## 8. Client responsibilities and exclusions

The client/owner should identify responsibility for business content, prices, product availability, delivery zones, tax/accounting rules, refund/cancellation policies, customer consent wording, provider accounts, legal compliance, customer support and timely approvals.

The final agreement must state exclusions and third-party dependencies clearly. This worksheet does not decide them.
